Tri dra da glask

1 · Grit anezhañ bras ha gorrek

War ar skramm kentañ, pouezit ur wech war ↓ evit kouezhañ war RATE. Dalc'hit C. An div nodenn a ziskenn izeloc'h-izelañ, betek na vint ket mui nodennoù met tik-tak disrannet, evel un horolaj graet gant mein. Dalc'hit D evit pignat en-dro betek ar youc'hadenn.

RATE a fiñv an div nodenn asambles : setu perak e chom ar patrom heñvel e-keit ma cheñch pep tra all. Ar c'hontroll fentusañ eo war ar mekanik.

2 · Grit ur ritm

War ar skramm kentañ pouezit war ↑ evit kouezhañ war MODE, neuze war A. ADVANCED a lavar bremañ hag ez eus muioc'h a skrammoù. Pouezit war 5 6 7 8 : an daboulin-droad, ar gaset hag an div charleston a zeu war-wel, hag e krog ur ritm. Pouezit war ] betek CLOCK hag implijit S ha X war BPM evit mont buanoc'h pe gorrekoc'h.

3 · Torrit anezhañ a-ratozh

Pouezit war ] betek DIRT. Pouezit war ↓ evit tizhout DRIVE ha dalc'hit D. Kreñv ha divalav e teu da vezañ. O a laka anezhañ er c'hreiz en-dro, I a lam anezhañ penn-da-benn.

C'est une machine qui fabrique du son avec deux notes qui se disputent. Il n'y a aucune chanson cachée dedans. C'est toi qui en fais une, en tournant des choses et en écoutant ce qui se passe.

Fais un son tout de suite

  1. Clique sur l'écran noir. Il n'écoute le clavier qu'après ça.
  2. Appuie sur Espace. Ça démarre et ça arrête.
  3. Appuie quatre ou cinq fois sur D : tout accélère. Maintenant appuie sur C jusqu'à ce que ça ralentisse.
  4. ] pour l'écran suivant, [ pour revenir en arrière.

Tu ne peux rien casser. Rien n'est enregistré, donc rien n'est précieux — si ça devient affreux, appuie sur Maj+O et cet écran repart de zéro.

Trois choses à essayer

1 · Rends-le énorme et lent

Sur le premier écran, appuie une fois sur ↓ pour te poser sur RATE. Garde C appuyé. Les deux notes descendent de plus en plus bas, jusqu'à ne plus être des notes du tout mais des tic-tac séparés, comme une horloge en gravier. Garde D appuyé pour remonter jusqu'au hurlement.

RATE déplace les deux notes ensemble : c'est pour ça que le motif reste le même pendant que tout le reste change. C'est la commande la plus amusante de la machine.

2 · Fabrique un rythme

Sur le premier écran, appuie sur ↑ pour te poser sur MODE, puis sur A. Ça affiche ADVANCED et il y a plus d'écrans. Appuie sur 5 6 7 8 : ça allume la grosse caisse, la caisse claire et les deux charlestons, et un rythme démarre. Appuie sur ] jusqu'à CLOCK et sers-toi de S et X sur BPM pour aller plus vite ou moins vite.

3 · Casse-le exprès

Appuie sur ] jusqu'à DIRT. Appuie sur ↓ pour atteindre DRIVE et garde D appuyé. Ça devient fort et mal élevé. O le remet au milieu, I l'enlève complètement.

La règle, pour toutes les commandes

Tous les écrans marchent pareil : quand tu sais ça, tu les sais tous. ↑ ↓ choisissent la ligne. ← → choisissent la chose sur cette ligne. Ni l'une ni l'autre ne change quoi que ce soit : elles ne font que désigner.

Ensuite, pour changer pour de vrai :

A Zun tout petit peu plus haut, un tout petit peu plus bas
S Xune quantité normale
D Cun grand saut
I O Paller directement en bas, au milieu, ou en haut

Ces six lettres sont côte à côte exprès : main gauche sur A S D, main droite sur I O P. Tu peux piloter toute la machine sans regarder le clavier.

Des mots qui font plus peur qu'ils ne devraient

oscillateurune chose qui fait une note. Cette machine en a deux, qui se disputent
comparateuril pose une seule question, sans arrêt : A est-il plus haut que B ? Son oui et son non, c'est de là que vient le rythme

La plupart des écrans ont une liste de tout ça sous les commandes principales : la source à gauche, combien au milieu, et ce que ça attrape à droite. De petites quantités font plus d'effet que tu ne crois — la course complète vaut vingt octaves.

This is a machine that makes sound out of two notes arguing with each other. There are no songs hidden inside it. You make one by turning things and listening to what happens.

Make a sound right now

  1. Click the black screen. It only listens to the keyboard once you do.
  2. Press Space. That starts it and stops it.
  3. Press D four or five times. Everything speeds up. Now press C until it slows back down.
  4. Press ] to go to the next screen, [ to go back.

You cannot break it. Nothing is saved, so nothing is precious — if it starts sounding horrible, press Shift+O and that screen goes back to nothing.

Three things to try

1 · Make it huge and slow

On the first screen, press ↓ once to land on RATE. Hold C. The two notes drop lower and lower until they stop being notes and turn into separate ticks, like a clock made of gravel. Hold D to take it back up into a scream.

RATE moves both notes together, which is why the pattern stays the same while everything else changes. It is the single most fun control on the machine.

2 · Make a beat

On the first screen press ↑ to land on MODE, then A. It now says ADVANCED and there are more screens. Press 5 6 7 8 — that switches on the kick, the snare and the two hats, and a beat starts. Press ] until you reach CLOCK and use S and X on BPM to make it faster or slower.

3 · Wreck it on purpose

Press ] until you reach DIRT. Press ↓ to reach DRIVE and hold D. It gets loud and rude. Press O to put it back in the middle, or I to take it all the way off.

The one rule for every control

Every screen works the same way, so once you know this you know all of them. ↑ ↓ choose which line you are on. ← → choose which thing on that line. Neither of them changes anything — they just point.

Then, to actually change it:

A Za tiny bit up, a tiny bit down
S Xa normal amount
D Ca big jump
I O Pjump straight to the bottom, the middle, or the top

Those six letters sit together on the keyboard on purpose: left hand on A S D, right hand on I O P. You can drive the whole machine without looking down.

Four machines, one at a time

MODE switches between them, and nothing is thrown away when you go back down — it is hidden until you switch up again.

CLASSICone screen: the original instrument's own panel. Two notes, the memory that scrambles them, one filter. Ten controls and no menus
PHOENIXJOLINone screen of dice. Each line is a part of the machine — R shakes that part, O puts it back. Play it by shaking
BENJOLIN17 screens: all of the above with every effect
ADVANCED24 screens: and a clock, two sequencers and four drums

The screens the two bigger ones have:

HOMEplay, speed, volume, and a bird that flaps along
CHAOSthe machine's imagination. It wanders about and pushes on everything else
OSC-1 OSC-2the two notes that do the arguing. Tune them against each other and the rhythm changes
COMPARATORthe referee. It asks "is this one higher than that one?" thousands of times a second, and its answers are the heartbeat
FUNCtwo envelope makers, like Tides. SLOPE puts the top of the shape where you want it, SHAPE says how it gets there (curved or straight), SMOOTH rounds the corners off or, the other way, breaks the shape into little bounces. AR follows the gate: a short gate makes a short envelope. AHR always finishes the rise, however short the trigger. CYCLE runs on its own. 0 to 10V. Under each one the shape is drawn as it actually comes out, the way the OSC and CHAOS pages do it. FN1 and FN2 turn up in the MOD banks.
FILTERthis is where the voice comes from. TYPE picks which filter: SVF the clean one (LP dark, HP thin, BP only the middle, NOTCH everything but the middle), ACID twice as steep and squelchy, VOWEL makes the machine say a, e, i, o, u, COMB turns the noise into plucked notes like a string, 1-BIT puts the comparator inside the filter so it locks and glitches, SCREAM lets the sound bend its own filter until it howls, and MORPH is one long sweep from low through the middle to high. The second word changes meaning with the first. Turn RES all the way up and most of them sing on their own — set IN to NONE and the filter is the note. The MOD rows underneath can drive all three: what FREQ does, what RES does, and the second word itself — so the machine can change voice, or vowel, in time with everything else
DIRTrude, broken, crunchy
FXswirly and wobbly
GLITCHgrabs a tiny piece of what just happened and repeats it
GRAINchops sound into little grains and throws handfuls of them
DELAYechoes
SPACEseven different places. ROOM is a room, PLATE is a sheet of metal singing, CLOUD is a fog with no direction in it, IRON is a slammed door, SHIMMER climbs every time round, and MI-CLOUD and MI-RINGS are the two reverbs out of the Mutable Instruments modules, exactly as they are. GATE chops the tail with one of the machine's own clocks — on NONE it chops nothing. Puts it in a room. A small one, a huge shiny one, or a slammed door. In SHIMMER the echo climbs an octave every time round: turn DECAY all the way up and it never stops — without running away either, it just holds at a level
MIXhow loud each sound is, and which effects it goes through
CHAINwhat order the effects happen in. Move the reverb before the dirt and see

Only on the big machine:

CLOCKa proper tempo in BPM. The small machine has none — there, the two notes decide the timing between them
SEQ-1 SEQ-2eight steps you can set by hand. Press Backspace on a step to make it a rest
DRUMSkick, snare, two hats. Each one picks what triggers it and how often it bothers

Words that sound scarier than they are

oscillatora thing that makes one note. This machine has two of them arguing
comparatorasks one question over and over: is A higher than B? Its yes and no is where the rhythm comes from
runglera small memory of eight yes/no answers going round in a circle. It never uses randomness, so a pattern you like stays found
attenuvertera dial for how much. Past the middle it goes backwards, so up becomes down
modulationone thing turning another thing's knob for you, over and over, faster than you could

Most screens have a list of these underneath the main controls: a source on the left, how much in the middle, and what it grabs hold of on the right. Small amounts do more than you expect — full travel is twenty octaves.
